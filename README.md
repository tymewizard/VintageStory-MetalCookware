# Metal Cookware 1.0.5

**Vintage Story:** 1.22.7 | **Mod ID:** `mc` | **Author:** tymewizard

Metal Cookware expands metal cookware, kitchenware, and food storage. Cast and forge bowls, cooking pots, crocks, crock lids, jugs and watering cans in a range of metals, with distinct appearances and vessel capacities. Includes support for additional high-tier forged cookware and optional integration with A Culinary Artillery.

## Installation

1. Back up your world before modifying an existing modded save.
2. Remove older Metal Cookware (`mc`) ZIP files from the Mods folder.
3. Install `MetalCookware_1.0.5_VS1.22.7.zip` without extracting it.
4. Install Expanded Matter (`em`) 3.8.3; this is a required dependency.
5. Start Vintage Story 1.22.7 and check the handbook for Metal Cookware recipes.

**Existing worlds:** No automatic migration/remapping from older `pewter:` or `metalpots:` content is bundled. Back up affected worlds and manage migrations separately.

## Features

- Castable and forged metal bowls, jugs, crock components, cooking pots, and watering cans.
- Iron, meteoric iron, steel, and titanium high-tier forged variants.
- Two finished forged crocks per 100-unit metal ingot (effective cost 50 units each).
- Metal-specific textures and item names, with capacity information shown in the handbook.
- Forgeable cookware variants and mold-based crafting.
- Optional A Culinary Artillery compatibility additions; ACA is not required.
- Expanded Matter integration for titanium-related materials.

## Capacity examples for forged cookware

| Metal | Crock | Cooking pot | Watering can |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Iron | 8 servings | 28 servings | 12 L |
| Meteoric iron | 9 servings | 30 servings | 14 L |
| Steel | 10 servings | 32 servings | 16 L |
| Titanium | 12 servings | 36 servings | 18 L |

Values reflect current gameplay definitions; not all pot/crock capacities have a fixed 4:1 relationship.
